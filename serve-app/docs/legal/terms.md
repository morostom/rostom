## 1. What SERVE is

SERVE is a platform that connects squash players in Egypt with clubs and
academies. Through SERVE you can find venues, see which courts are free, book
court time, follow your club's or academy's schedule, and keep a player card.

**SERVE is an intermediary.** Courts, coaching and training sessions are
provided by the club or academy, not by SERVE. SERVE does not employ coaches,
operate courts, or supervise sessions.

## 2. Accounts

You need an account to book a court or join a venue. You agree to give
accurate information and to keep your password to yourself. You are
responsible for what happens under your account.

You must be at least [MINIMUM AGE] years old to hold your own SERVE account.
If you are younger, a parent or guardian must hold the account and manage it
for you.

## 3. Children and parent accounts

SERVE is used by junior players. Where a player is under 18:

- A parent or guardian may create a parent account and link to their child.
- The link only takes effect once the child approves it from their own app.
- A parent can see their child's sessions and bookings, and can approve
  payment requests the child sends them.
- Where a player is under 16, cancelling a session requires parental approval.

By creating an account for a child, or by approving a link to a child, you
confirm you are that child's parent or legal guardian and that you consent to
SERVE processing their information as described in the Privacy Policy.

## 4. Bookings and cancellations

When you book a court through SERVE, your agreement for that court time is
with the venue. The venue sets its own prices, opening hours and cancellation
terms. SERVE shows you what the venue has published.

A venue may cancel or change a session. SERVE will tell you as soon as it
knows, but SERVE is not responsible for a venue's decision.

## 5. Safety, supervision and injury

Squash is a physical sport and carries a risk of injury.

- The venue is responsible for the condition of its courts and facilities.
- The venue is responsible for supervising sessions and for the conduct,
  qualifications and vetting of its coaches and staff.
- SERVE does not verify coaching qualifications, background checks, insurance
  or facility safety, and does not hold itself out as doing so.
- You are responsible for deciding whether a session is appropriate for you or
  your child.

SERVE may remove a review that is abusive, false, or not about the venue.
You may edit or delete your own review; you cannot edit anyone else's.
