### 1 · WHAT SERVE IS

SERVE is a squash platform built for Egypt — the country that dominates world squash yet still runs its clubs on paper schedules, WhatsApp threads, and phone calls. SERVE digitises the entire squash ecosystem into three connected surfaces that share one live, realtime backbone:

1. **The Player App** (mobile) — every player gets a collectible-style **Player Card** (competitive or recreational) that levels up through five tiers as they book and play: **Beginner (bronze) → Amateur (silver) → Semi-pro (red) → Pro (gold) → Elite (dark blue)**. Players see their club's courts live, book and pay in-app, discover academies across Egypt with ratings and reviews, and manage everything in English or Arabic (full right-to-left).

2. **The Club Console** (desktop) — for members-only clubs like Heliopolis Sporting Club. A coordinator runs a **live court board** (mark a court busy on the desk, and every member's phone updates within a second), a mix-and-match **schedule builder** that publishes straight to players' apps, coach management, member **access codes** (clubs are private; members unlock them with a code), and **multi-branch support** — e.g. Heliopolis runs *Masr El Gedida (7 courts)* and *El Shorouk (10 courts)*, each with its own live board.

3. **The Academy Console** (desktop) — for open-booking academies like Ramy Ashour Squash Academy. Owners get a dashboard, coach and schedule management, and a **brand studio**: upload a logo and cover photo, pick a brand colour, and the academy's entire presence — console and player-facing pages — re-themes instantly.

**The connective tissue (SERVE's signature):** everything is live. An edit made once appears everywhere, instantly — court status, schedules, coaches, branding. No refresh, no sync button.

**The family layer (SERVE's most human feature):** the **Parent Account**. Parents don't get a player card — they get their child. Link to your child (the child must approve the link from their own phone — consent built in), see their training schedule, and book courts for them. When a child reaches checkout, they can tap **"Transfer to parent"**: the court is held for **10 minutes** while the parent's phone gets a real alert — banner, sound, vibration — to approve and pay from wherever they are. No more "call mom to check the app." Under-16s who want to cancel a training session need parent approval too — same alert flow. And if a parent gets the itch to play, one tap ("Feeling groovy?") builds them their own player card to challenge the other parents.

**Safety-net details worth surfacing:** cancelling a court booking gives a 60-second undo; cancelling a club session gives a 15-minute undo and notifies the club's coordinator and coach automatically. Players and parents can message an academy's owner or head coach directly over WhatsApp from inside the app. Academy ratings and reviews create healthy competition between academies — better service rises to the top of Discover, which sorts by **Near me / Best / Courts**.

### 2 · BRAND SYSTEM (non-negotiable)

- **Canvas:** near-black `#070707`; surfaces `#121212`; hairline borders `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`
- **Primary accent — neon red:** `#f5453b` (the SERVE signature; use for emphasis, glows, active states, the wordmark)
- **Club blue:** `#4ea8ff` (Heliopolis's colour; secondary accent, use sparingly)
- **Support palette:** court green `#4ade80`, gold `#d4a64f`, violet `#a779f0`
- **Tier colours:** bronze `#cd7f32` · silver `#c0c0c0` · red `#f5453b` · gold `#d4a64f` · dark blue `#1e40af`
- **Type:** display/headlines **Space Grotesk** (bold, tight letter-spacing, large); body **Geist** (or Inter as fallback); labels/eyebrows **JetBrains Mono**, uppercase, letter-spaced (this mono-eyebrow pattern is a core SERVE signature — use it on every slide: small uppercase mono label above each headline)
- **Texture:** subtle glow behind accent elements; thin court-line motifs (a squash court's service box lines make an elegant background grid); generous negative space; never cluttered
- **Wordmark:** "SERVE" in Space Grotesk bold with a neon-red glow, often paired with "EST. 2026" in tiny mono
- **Mood:** premium, athletic, nocturnal — a night match under floodlights. Think Nike Training Club meets a trading terminal, never corporate SaaS.

### 4 · RULES

- **Never** mention pricing, monetisation, business model, market size, competitors, or fundraising. This deck introduces a product.
- Use the real Egyptian context confidently: Heliopolis Sporting Club, Ramy Ashour Squash Academy, Masr El Gedida, El Shorouk, TELDA — these are the product's world, not placeholders.
- Every slide: dark canvas, one mono uppercase eyebrow, one Space Grotesk headline, minimal body copy. Let the screenshots and typography carry it.
- Keep all screenshot placeholder frames **empty and labeled with their slot ID** so the real PNGs can be dropped in at exact size.
- Aim for the confidence of an Apple keynote and the texture of a night-match broadcast.
